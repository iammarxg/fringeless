# Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Fringeless does not collect, send, sell or share any data. It has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no remote code, and it makes no network requests.

## What it stores

Your settings: looks, rules for sites (the addresses you add), rules for screens (a name and size), and theme. They are kept in your browser's extension storage.

If you turn on **Sync**, your browser copies these settings between your own devices through your browser account. Fringeless never receives them. Sync is off by default. **Backup** writes a file only when you ask for one.

Removing the extension removes its settings.

## What each permission is for

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Access to sites (optional, you grant it) | Adding the styles that change how text is drawn. Page content is not read, stored or sent. |
| `storage` | Keeping your settings |
| `scripting` | Adding the styles to pages you allowed |
| `activeTab` | Acting on the current tab when you open the popup or use a shortcut |
| Window management (optional, asked by **Detect my screens**) | Reading the names and sizes of your connected screens to find the model |

**Check this page** takes a picture of the visible tab when you press it, counts colored pixels on your computer, and discards it.
