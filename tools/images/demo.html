Why small text has colored edges

Reading on an OLED monitor, part two

Clear little letters will still look soft when every stem carries a red edge on one side and a blue edge on the other.

Subpixel smoothing borrows the red, green and blue parts of each pixel to draw thinner shapes. It expects those parts in one order. When a panel uses another, the color ends up beside the letter and not inside it.

What to look for

Find a word with many straight stems, such as illicit, millilitre or Illinois, and look closely at the left and right edges.

On white text over black the edges are usually easier to see. Headings show it too, as a faint glow along each vertical stroke.

What helps

Drawing the same letters in shades of gray removes the color. The strokes may look a little lighter or heavier, so it helps to adjust the weight until it reads well to you.
